# Four-quiz localisation audit — in progress (23 September 2026)

Scope: Years Left, Memory, Treatments and Vision in English plus 29 translated locales (120 editions). This is a working audit record, **not a release sign-off or a claim of native-language perfection**.

## Corrections made in this pass

- Years Left: aligned the two-line landing promise with “One surprising result. What age will you get?”; made result disclaimers explicitly say that the quiz does not predict lifespan where translations were incomplete or literal.
- Memory: rewrote machine-like introductions in the affected locales, including mistranslations of “memory-area breakdown”, “working memory” and the rewarded-ad flow; corrected several disclaimer grammar errors.
- Treatments: changed result disclaimers that only denied measuring healthcare “fitness” to the stronger intended statement that the quiz does **not qualify anyone to provide healthcare**. Replaced untranslated Hungarian medical answer choices. Where the question asks for the coronary-bypass *procedure*, changed answers that named only a graft or implant. Fixed the Romanian physiotherapy answer so it names the same treatment as the question.
- Vision: corrected literal or ungrammatical eye-care disclaimers, including Swedish agreement and “vision states” wording in several languages.
- Shared Portuguese `/pt`: corrected machine-like result profiles and instructions in all four families, clarified several Treatments questions, and made the Memory recall prompts agree with their study board. The application deliberately serves **one shared Portuguese locale**; its validation rejects several Portugal-only and Brazil-only terms. The existing `Comboio / trem` memory clue remains a deliberate dual term, because changing just one occurrence would break the study/answer correspondence. This is not the same as a fully native `pt-PT` or `pt-BR` edition.
- French `/fr`: reviewed the full user-facing text of the four quiz files. Treatments had a mixed `vous`/`tu` instruction, literal expressions such as “séparez les sosies”, awkward result labels and several question-answer grammar errors. Memory asked for Maya's *pelage* although the study board showed her *manteau*; that meaning error is fixed. Vision had nonsensical result copy (including a false translation of “miss at a glance”) and ambiguous colour-label wording. Years Left had one `votre` question among otherwise `tu` copy. These were corrected without moving answer positions. The French medical terms *kinésithérapie*, *orthophonie* and *pontage coronarien* were checked against [Assurance Maladie guidance on kinésithérapie](https://www.ameli.fr/node/554702), [its orthophonie nomenclature](https://www.ameli.fr/sites/default/files/Documents/NGAP-09042025.pdf) and [its coronary-treatment guidance](https://www.ameli.fr/assure/sante/themes/angine-de-poitrine-angor/traitement-medical).
- German `/de`: reviewed all four quiz files and corrected literal introductions/results, inconsistent formal vs informal address, clinical terminology and multiple unnatural questions and answer fragments. Treatments now asks for the therapy field when the answer is *Logopädie*, and uses *Palliativversorgung* for care rather than *Palliativpflege* alone. Memory asks about Maya's *Mantel*, not her fur, to match the study board. Vision puzzle labels and accessibility descriptions were simplified. Clinical wording was cross-checked against [German patient guidance on Logopädie](https://www.gesundheitsinformation.de/was-ist-eine-logopaedie.html), [palliative care](https://www.gesundheitsinformation.de/sterben-tod-und-trauer.html) and [coronary bypass](https://gesund.bund.de/glossar/aortokoronarer-bypass).
- Italian `/it`: reviewed all four quiz files. Treatments contained answer choices phrased as commands rather than descriptions, a profession-versus-therapy mismatch, English `CBT` instead of the Italian `TCC`, and machine-like checkpoint/result copy. Years Left had literal or nonsensical future-self questions and result profiles; Memory had a CTA mismatch and asked about a *sacchetto* where the study board showed a *borsa*; Vision result profiles contained mistranslations including “traps of the latest generation” and “interest profile.” These were corrected while preserving question IDs and answer positions. Italian medical wording was checked against [Istituto Superiore di Sanità usage of TCC](https://publ.iss.it/ITA/Items/GetPDF?uuid=a2c6bc46-3bca-427e-9e6d-537573dcf683), [its material on logopedists and swallowing](https://www.iss.it/documents/20126/45616/08-38.1232458810.pdf/2d952d3c-85eb-ded7-7917-ef10ec9bbdd4?t=1581094968644) and [Ministero della Salute guidance on palliative care](https://www.salute.gov.it/new/it/tema/nuova-assistenza-distrettuale/rete-delle-cure-palliative/).
- Memory cross-locale recall: compared every translation of Maya's coat question against its study board and corrected mismatched or ungrammatical lines in Czech, Greek, Finnish, Croatian, Norwegian, Polish, Romanian, Slovak, Serbian, Swedish, Thai, Turkish and Ukrainian. The underlying study data and answer keys were not changed.

Years Left questions and responses now use consistent informal address; Vision result copy and accessibility descriptions were clarified. Clinical terminology was checked against [NCI's Spanish immunotherapy explanation](https://www.cancer.gov/espanol/cancer/tratamiento/tipos/inmunoterapia). ## Verification boundaries

- Schema, key, answer-position and build checks establish completeness and mechanical consistency; they do not establish native fluency.
- Mobile browser journeys establish that content renders and the five-round flow works; they do not establish that every question has its intended meaning in every language.
- A numeric-token comparison found no unexplained number change in Treatments; the differences sampled in the other three families were spelled-out numbers, local time formatting or visual-puzzle instructions. A normalised duplicate-answer scan found no accidental duplicate answer labels; Vision’s dot-counting choices are intentionally different lengths.
