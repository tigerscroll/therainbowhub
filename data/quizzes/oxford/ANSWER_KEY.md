# English oxford answer key

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-english-chapters.mjs oxford`. Headlines and landing subtitles are copied verbatim from the original English version. Each topic contains seven questions; answer positions are deterministically shuffled and balanced across the quiz.

Questions use plain English for UK, AU, CA and US readers. Quantities and times state their units; supplied examples are not universal clinical, workshop or food-service requirements. All questions are authored and self-contained. Institutions, rules, rates and scenarios in the questions are hypothetical. The answer key explains each calculation or deduction. Currency examples use stated credits; times and measurements specify their units.

The links below are topic references for the factual principles; unlinked entries are authored general knowledge or reasoning from the facts in the question.
