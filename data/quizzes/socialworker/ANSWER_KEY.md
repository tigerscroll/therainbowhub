Rebuild with `node scripts/build-english-chapters.mjs socialworker`. Headlines and landing subtitles are copied verbatim from the original English version. Each topic contains seven questions; answer positions are deterministically shuffled and balanced across the quiz.

Questions use plain English for UK, AU, CA and US readers. Quantities and times state their units; supplied examples are not universal clinical, workshop or food-service requirements. An independent knowledge challenge, not an official entrance examination or professional qualification. Safeguarding actions depend on local law and service procedures; these questions test general professional reasoning.
