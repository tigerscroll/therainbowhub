# English airforce answer key

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-english-chapters.mjs airforce`. Headlines and landing subtitles are copied verbatim from the original English version. Each topic contains seven questions; answer positions are deterministically shuffled and balanced across the quiz.

Questions use plain English for UK, AU, CA and US readers. Quantities and times state their units; supplied examples are not universal clinical, workshop or food-service requirements. An independent knowledge challenge, not an official entrance examination or professional qualification. This tests civilian aviation principles, not military selection rules. Calculation examples are not flight-planning instructions.

The links below are topic references for the factual principles; unlinked entries are authored general knowledge or reasoning from the facts in the question.
