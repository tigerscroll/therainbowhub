The links below are topic references for the factual principles; unlinked entries are authored general knowledge or reasoning from the facts in the question. The retained question bank comes from the project’s reviewed worldwide Mechanic edition. See [its manufacturer and safety references](../SOURCES.md). Added questions use broad component functions and self-contained measurements; no DIY high-voltage procedures are included.
