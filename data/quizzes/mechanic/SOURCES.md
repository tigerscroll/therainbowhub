# Mechanic English edition — 22 September 2026 UTC

Five rounds of six original/adapted text questions. The supplied values in Workshop Logic are hypothetical examples, not universal service limits. Diagnostic scenarios ask for supported suspects or checks, not guaranteed diagnoses or DIY repair procedures.

## Worldwide English review

- No national road rules, inspection programmes, legal tread-depth limits, currencies or driving-side assumptions.
- Conventional fuel-powered cars are identified where an alternator/charging-system assumption matters. Electric cars are not treated as mechanically identical to them.
- General dashboard conventions are qualified with “commonly”; the manual governs a particular vehicle.
- Numerical questions supply the relevant units, targets and ratios. The reduction-ratio question uses rpm; the fuel question concerns a test **car**, not an engine travelling a distance.
- Example coolant concentration and component thickness are not recommendations for a real vehicle.
- The 80% challenge is a quiz target, not a claim about population pass rates. Unsupported “Only 7%” and participant-count claims are no longer displayed.
