# Mechanic quiz review — 23 September 2026 UTC

One stage of ten selected text questions, available in English and 29 translated locales. The retained question IDs and header labels reflect the former five-round source set; they do not create multiple quiz rounds. The supplied coolant-mix value is a hypothetical training example, not a universal service recommendation. Diagnostic scenarios ask for supported suspects or checks, not guaranteed diagnoses or DIY repair procedures.

## Worldwide content review

- No national road rules, inspection programmes, legal tread-depth limits, currencies or driving-side assumptions.
- Electric cars are not treated as mechanically identical to conventional fuel-powered cars; many retain a separate low-voltage battery.
- General dashboard conventions are qualified with “commonly”; the manual governs a particular vehicle.
- The coolant-mix arithmetic supplies its own volume and concentration; its example value is not a recommendation for a real vehicle.
- The 80% challenge is a quiz target, not a claim about population pass rates. Unsupported “Only 7%” and participant-count claims are no longer displayed.
