# English iq answer key

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-english-chapters.mjs iq`. Headlines and landing subtitles are copied verbatim from the original English version. Each topic contains seven questions; answer positions are deterministically shuffled and balanced across the quiz.

Questions use plain English for UK, AU, CA and US readers. Quantities and times state their units; supplied examples are not universal clinical, workshop or food-service requirements. All puzzles are authored. The answer key states the rule or deduction for every question. Alphabet puzzles use the English A–Z alphabet; directions refer to the displayed page or the compass directions stated. No cultural trivia, currencies or regional word meanings are required.

The links below are topic references for the factual principles; unlinked entries are authored general knowledge or reasoning from the facts in the question.
